About Research Experience Honors

Hao Yu 余昊

I’m a second-year master student at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Sida Peng and Prof. Xiaowei Zhou. I'm also a Tencent Project Up research intern at Tencent Hunyuan, building 3D world model. I value simple but effective ideas—a philosophy captured by the Chinese saying, “A heavy sword needs no sharp edge; true mastery needs no ornament.”

Over the past two years, I developed the InfiniDepth and InfiniSplat series for feed-forward 3D reconstruction, which serve as simulation environments for autonomous driving and robot navigation. Looking ahead, I want to work directly on the core challenges in building physical agents, with the hope that they will one day become as useful in the physical world as LLM agents are in the digital world today.
